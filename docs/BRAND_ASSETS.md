# Latchmail brand assets

The supplied brand images live under `src/web/assets/brand/` with stable, role-based names:

- `latchmail-mark.png` — transparent square mark used in authenticated navigation.
- `latchmail-wordmark.png` — transparent horizontal logo used on the sign-in card.
- `latchmail-hero.png` — wide brand artwork used as the sign-in card's supporting visual on desktop.

The checked-in assets are the complete runtime inputs; Vite fingerprints and bundles them during production builds.
